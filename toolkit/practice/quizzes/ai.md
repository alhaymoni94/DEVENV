# AI Quiz

Q: How do you ask a one-shot AI question?
A: ai "your question"

Q: How do you pipe file contents to the AI?
A: cat file.py | ai "your prompt"

Q: How do you start an interactive AI session?
A: ai or opencode

Q: How do you use a system prompt?
A: ai --system "you are an expert" "your question"

Q: How do you save AI output to a file?
A: ai "question" > answer.md

Q: How do you exit an interactive session?
A: Ctrl+D or /exit

Q: How do you see all available slash commands in opencode?
A: /help

Q: Where is the opencode configuration stored?
A: ~/.opencode.json

Q: How would you ask the AI to explain a Python script?
A: cat script.py | ai "explain this code"

Q: What's the difference between `ai` and `opencode`?
A: ai is for one-shot questions and pipe input; opencode is a full coding agent that reads your codebase
