# Micro Quiz

Q: How do you save a file in micro?
A: Ctrl+S

Q: How do you quit micro?
A: Ctrl+Q

Q: How do you undo a change?
A: Ctrl+Z

Q: How do you redo a change?
A: Ctrl+Y

Q: How do you find text?
A: Ctrl+F

Q: How do you find and replace?
A: Ctrl+H

Q: How do you copy, cut, and paste?
A: Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V

Q: How do you toggle comments?
A: Ctrl+/

Q: How do you duplicate the current line?
A: Ctrl+D

Q: How do you go to a specific line number?
A: Ctrl+G

Q: How do you add multiple cursors?
A: Alt+Click on each location

Q: How do you collapse multiple cursors back to one?
A: Escape

Q: How do you open micro with the file browser?
A: e .

Q: What alias opens micro?
A: e

Q: What environment variable sets micro as the default editor?
A: EDITOR=micro
