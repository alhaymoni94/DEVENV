# Shell Quiz

Q: How do you search command history interactively?
A: Ctrl+R

Q: How do you go to the start of the line?
A: Ctrl+A

Q: How do you go to the end of the line?
A: Ctrl+E

Q: How do you delete the word before the cursor?
A: Ctrl+W

Q: How do you delete to the start of the line?
A: Ctrl+U

Q: How do you delete to the end of the line?
A: Ctrl+K

Q: How do you clear the screen?
A: Ctrl+L

Q: How do you rerun the last command with sudo?
A: sudo !!

Q: How do you cancel a running command?
A: Ctrl+C

Q: How do you suspend a process and resume it later?
A: Ctrl+Z to suspend, fg to resume

Q: What does `..` do in this shell config?
A: cd's up one directory (AUTO_CD is enabled)

Q: How do you jump forward one word?
A: Alt+F

Q: What alias opens the micro editor?
A: e

Q: What alias opens lazygit?
A: lg

Q: How do you remove a specific entry from history?
A: history -d <line-number>
