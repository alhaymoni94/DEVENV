# Lazygit Quiz

Q: What alias opens lazygit?
A: lg

Q: How do you stage or unstage a file?
A: Space

Q: How do you stage all files at once?
A: a

Q: How do you write a commit message?
A: c, then type message, then Enter

Q: How do you push to the remote?
A: P (uppercase)

Q: How do you pull from the remote?
A: p (lowercase)

Q: How do you undo the last git action?
A: z

Q: How do you create a new branch?
A: n, then type branch name

Q: How do you amend the last commit?
A: A (after staging changes)

Q: How do you view all keybindings?
A: ?

Q: How do you cycle between panels?
A: Tab

Q: How do you switch tabs within a panel?
A: [ or ]

Q: How do you squash a commit?
A: s (in the commits panel)

Q: How do you revert a commit?
A: t (in the commits panel)

Q: How do you cherry-pick a commit?
A: c (in the commits panel)
