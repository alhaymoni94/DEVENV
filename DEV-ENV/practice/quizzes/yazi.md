# Yazi Quiz (CUA)

Q: How do you open yazi with the cd-on-quit wrapper?
A: y

Q: How do you navigate up/down in yazi?
A: ↑ / ↓

Q: How do you go to the parent directory?
A: ←

Q: How do you copy selected files?
A: Ctrl+C

Q: How do you paste files?
A: Ctrl+V

Q: How do you trash selected files?
A: d

Q: How do you create a new file or directory?
A: a

Q: How do you rename a file?
A: r

Q: How do you quit and cd to the current directory?
A: Ctrl+Q

Q: How do you search for a filename?
A: /

Q: How do you toggle hidden files?
A: .

Q: How do you select all files?
A: V

Q: How do you open a new tab?
A: Ctrl+T

Q: How do you jump to a previously visited directory?
A: z (zoxide)

Q: How do you bulk rename selected files?
A: R (opens names in editor)
